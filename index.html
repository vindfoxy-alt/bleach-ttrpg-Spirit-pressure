What is it?

Spirit Pressure takes the d20 rules you already know and swaps the fuel. You have Hit Points for your body and Reiatsu Points (RP) for your spiritual pressure. Flash steps, released swords, arrows of light and energy blasts all cost RP, so every fight is a choice about when to burn it.

Familiar core

d20 + modifier vs AC, six ability scores, advantage and disadvantage, saving throws, death saves.

Two resources

HP keeps you standing. RP powers your techniques. Short rests return half of it, long rests all of it.

Ultimate forms

At level 11 every class unlocks a signature ultimate: Bankai, Vollständig, a Full Mask, a Complete.

Four classes

Each class picks a subclass at level 2 that shapes its techniques and its level 11 ultimate form.

Changelog

v0.2 · 27 Nov 2026

  • Every subclass is now named after a character from the series (Yamamoto, Aizen, Zaraki, Urahara, Uryū Ishida, Bazz-B, Shinji Hirako, Orihime and more).
  • The rulebook is now two books: a Player's Handbook and a Game Master's Guide.
