What is it?

Spirit Pressure takes the d20 rules you already know and swaps the fuel. You have Hit Points for your body and Reiatsu Points (RP) for your spiritual pressure. Flash steps, released swords, arrows of light and energy blasts all cost RP, so every fight is a choice about when to burn it.

Familiar core

d20 + modifier vs AC, six ability scores, advantage and disadvantage, saving throws, death saves.

Two resources

HP keeps you standing. RP powers your techniques. Short rests return half of it, long rests all of it.

Ultimate forms

At level 11 every class unlocks a signature ultimate: Bankai, Vollständig, a Full Mask, a Complete.

Four classes

Each class picks a subclass at level 2 that shapes its techniques and its level 11 ultimate form.

Changelog

v0.6 · 27 Nov 2026

  • Kidō reworked: three voices (full chant, spoken, cut chant) and Weaving, where casting two different schools in one turn gives the second one a free chant. Every Soul Reaper subclass now changes how you cast.
  • New spells, including Mass Restoration and the first Forbidden Kidō (Kuyō Shibari, Kurohitsugi, Sajō Sabaku, Inochi Gaeshi). Each has a Toll the caster must pay and its own special effect.
  • Creator: pick your Kidō (Soul Reapers), copy a share link, save in this browser, and subclass names link to the handbook.
  • Healing now scales with level (Benihime Reversal, Soten Kisshun, Circle and Mass Restoration).
  • Balance pass from the simulator: Hollowborn d10 hit die, flat damage reduction in place of resistance and a 4 RP Cero; Quincy Piercing Light and a third attack at 18; Quincy and Fullbringer d10 hit die.
  • GM Guide: more enemies (Gillian, Espada-style duelist, Forbidden Kidō Master and others), seven new relics, advice on teaching Forbidden Kidō and a starter adventure, The Night the Gate Opened.

v0.5 · 27 Nov 2026

  • New Fight Simulator: pick a subclass and level, choose how the enemies behave (brute, focus, assassin, hunter or bully) and run a thousand fights, compare all 16 subclasses, or watch one fight round by round.
  • Subclasses reworked to match the series: Aizen (Kyōka Suigetsu), Zaraki (Nozarashi), Urahara (Benihime), Riruka (Dollhouse), Tsukishima, Ginjō, Orihime, Haschwalth, Grimmjow, Yammy and Ulquiorra. Yamamoto is now fire only.
  • Quincy: the Reishi Bow hits with DEX, damage adds INT, range 120/360.
  • Creator: feature names link to the Player's Handbook, skills show their ability, and a Recommended build button fills in your class's best scores (★ best, ◆ second).
  • First balance pass from a fight simulator (in the tools folder): Hollowborn AC is 10 + DEX + half CON and Hollow Vitality heals half proficiency; Fullbringer Bond Weapon is now 1d10; Yamamoto's fire wall deals 1d8 and the burning is removed.
  • Support subclasses are stronger: Orihime (3d6 healing, 50 HP shield), Haschwalth (2d8 Warding Bow, cheaper dome, 3d6 returned damage), Urahara (4d6 healing, 1d8 domain healing) and Tsukishima (your allies also get advantage from Insertion).

v0.4 · 27 Nov 2026

  • Every subclass now gives two permanent skill proficiencies at level 2, and each background lets you choose 2 of its 5 skills.
  • Creator: ability scores by standard array, point buy (27 points), or roll 4d6 and assign the results yourself.
  • New Character Creator: pick class, subclass, ancestry and background, assign scores, and get HP, RP, AC, saves, skills and features for levels 1-20. Save and load with a character code, or print the sheet.

v0.3 · 27 Nov 2026

  • New hook page and a "Play in 5 Minutes" quick start with a ready-made example character.
  • One-page poster card for each class, with how it feels to play, who will love it and the first five levels. Art spaces are ready for illustrations.
  • "In play" example boxes, shorter rules text and dark pages.

v0.2 · 27 Nov 2026

  • Every subclass is now named after a character from the series (Yamamoto, Aizen, Zaraki, Urahara, Uryū Ishida, Bazz-B, Shinji Hirako, Orihime and more).
  • The rulebook is now two books: a Player's Handbook and a Game Master's Guide.
  • Bigger text and bolder page design.
  • Subclasses are laid out on one colour-coded page per class, with level 2, 6, 11 and 17 in rows.

v0.1 draft · 27 Nov 2026

  • First core rulebook: core rules, four classes with four subclasses each, Kidō, gear, enemies, GM chapter and a blank character sheet.
  • Working title. Balance has not been playtested yet.
