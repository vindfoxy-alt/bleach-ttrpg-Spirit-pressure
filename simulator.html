Set up the fight

Your hero fights beside three plain Soul Reaper fighters.

Enemy personalities

Brute
attacks anyone at random
Focus
finishes whoever has the lowest HP percentage
Assassin
goes for the squishiest (lowest AC)
Hunter
chases whoever has dealt the most damage
Bully
attacks whoever has the most HP left
Healing and protection matter most against Focus and Hunter enemies, which concentrate damage on one person.

Result

Press Run to simulate. The party is your hero plus three plain Soul Reaper fighters, against a boss and two minions. Difficulty is tuned so a plain party has the same chance against every enemy personality, so the results show who copes better with each.
